# Old vs. New Tax Regime: FY 2025-26 Comparison Guide

The Union Budget FY 2025-26 solidified the **New Tax Regime** as the default choice with lower slab rates and a **₹75,000 standard deduction**.

## Breakeven Point Strategy
Old Tax Regime becomes better ONLY when your total claimed deductions (Section 80C + 24b Home Loan Interest + 80D + HRA) exceed **₹3,75,000 to ₹4,25,000** annually.
