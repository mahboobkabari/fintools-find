# SIP vs. Lumpsum Investment: Ultimate Financial Guide

Choosing between **Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)** and **Lumpsum Investment** is one of the most critical decisions for mutual fund investors.

## 1. Understanding the Core Difference

- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**: Deploys a fixed sum of money at regular intervals (monthly/quarterly), benefiting from rupee cost averaging.
- **Lumpsum Investment**: Deploys a single one-time lump sum into mutual funds on Day 1.

## 2. Real-World Case Study

Suppose you have **₹1,20,000** to invest over 1 year:
- **Strategy A (SIP)**: Invest ₹10,000 every month for 12 months.
- **Strategy B (Lumpsum)**: Invest ₹1,20,000 upfront on Day 1.

If the market drops 15% in Month 3 and recovers by Month 12, **SIP produces higher net units** because monthly installments buy extra units at discount prices.
