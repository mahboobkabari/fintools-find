# Car Loan vs. Personal Loan: Vehicle Financing Guide

Financed vehicle purchases account for over 75% of new cars sold in India. Deciding between a **Secured Car Loan** and an **Unsecured Personal Loan** determines your monthly EMI burden.

## Quick Recommendation
- **New Car Purchase**: Always choose a **Car Loan** (8.5%–9.5% interest rate).
- **Older Used Car Purchase**: Consider a **Personal Loan** if used car loan rates exceed 13% p.a.
