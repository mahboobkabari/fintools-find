# NPS vs. PPF: Ultimate Retirement Comparison Guide

Both **National Pension System (NPS)** and **Public Provident Fund (PPF)** are flagship government-backed retirement instruments in India.

## Key Strategy
- **Choose NPS** if you are under age 45 and want equity compounding to beat inflation.
- **Choose PPF** if you seek 100% guaranteed risk-free EEE tax exemption.
