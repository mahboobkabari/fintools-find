# Home Loan vs. Personal Loan: Institutional Comparison

When financing major life goals, choosing between a **Home Loan** and a **Personal Loan** determines your financial health for years.

## Key Takeaway
For property purchases or home construction, **always choose a Home Loan**. Personal loans should strictly be reserved for urgent unsecured liquidity needs.
