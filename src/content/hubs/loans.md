# Institutional Guide to Loans & Debt Management

Borrowing money is one of the most consequential financial decisions you will ever make. Taking the wrong loan or selecting an improper tenure can lock you into paying lakhs of rupees in unnecessary interest.

## 1. The Core Pillars of Smart Borrowing

To maintain financial health while taking a loan, always enforce three rules:

1. **Keep FOIR below 40%**: Your total monthly EMIs across all loans should never exceed 40% of your take-home pay.
2. **Prioritize Secured Debt**: Opt for secured loans (Home/Car) with collateral rather than high-interest personal credit.
3. **Prepay Early**: Making small lump-sum prepayments during the first 5 years of a long-term loan yields maximum compounding interest savings.

## 2. Types of Loan Instruments

- **Home Loans**: Longest tenure (up to 30 years), lowest interest rates (~8.5%), with tax deductions under Section 24(b) and 80C.
- **Car Loans**: Secured vehicle financing (8.5%–9.5%) with up to 90% LTV on-road coverage.
- **Personal Loans**: 100% unsecured instant cash (12%–18%) for urgent liquidity, but with higher interest costs.
- **Education Loans**: Dedicated student financing with tax deductions on interest under Section 80E.
