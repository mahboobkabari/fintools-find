## Mastering the 50/30/20 Budgeting Framework

The **50/30/20 Budgeting Rule** is one of the most effective personal cash flow management frameworks in modern personal finance. Rather than forcing you to track every penny across dozens of micro-categories, it establishes three clear, intuitive spending buckets:

$$\text{Net Monthly Income} = \text{Needs (50\%)} + \text{Wants (30\%)} + \text{Savings (20\%)}$$

---

### Core Budget Frameworks Comparison

| Budgeting Framework | Needs Target | Wants Target | Savings Target | Ideal Target Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard 50/30/20** | **50%** | **30%** | **20%** | Balanced lifestyle & consistent wealth compounding |
| **Metro Living 60/20/20** | **60%** | **20%** | **20%** | High rent & cost of living in tier-1 metro cities |
| **Debt Recovery 70/20/10** | **70%** | **20%** | **10%** | Heavy debt repayment & essential living focus |
| **Aggressive FIRE 40/20/40** | **40%** | **20%** | **40%** | Early retirement & rapid financial independence |

---

### The Power of the 20% Savings Rule

Consistently investing **20% of your monthly salary** into diversified equity mutual funds compounding at an assumed **12% annual CAGR** transforms monthly cash flow into multi-decade financial security:

$$\text{Future Corpus} = S_{\text{monthly}} \times \left[ \frac{(1 + r/12)^{n} - 1}{r/12} \right] \times (1 + r/12)$$

For example, a **₹20,000 monthly investment** grows into **₹46.5 Lakhs in 10 years**, **₹1.0 Crore in 15 years**, and **₹2.0 Crores in 20 years**.
