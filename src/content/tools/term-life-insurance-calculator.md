## Understanding Term Life Insurance Sizing & Actuarial Protection

**Term Life Insurance** is the foundation of personal financial risk management. Unlike complex endowment, money-back, or unit-linked insurance plans (ULIPs), term insurance is **pure financial protection**: it provides maximum life cover for the lowest possible annual premium. If the breadwinner passes away prematurely, the life insurance company pays a guaranteed, tax-free lump sum to the family to eliminate debts, sustain household living standards, and fund long-term life goals.

---

### Comparison of Life Insurance Sizing Frameworks

| Sizing Framework | Mathematical Formula | Best Suited Profile | Primary Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DIME Needs-Based** | $\text{Debts} + (\text{Expenses} \times Y) + \text{Goals} - \text{Assets}$ | Married couples & parents with loans | Precise liability and goal coverage |
| **Human Life Value (HLV)** | $E_{\text{net}} \times \left[\frac{1 - (1 + r)^{-n}}{r}\right]$ | High-earning professionals & executives | Replaces total lifetime economic value |
| **Income Multiple Rule** | $15\text{x} - 25\text{x} \times \text{Annual Gross Income}$ | Quick initial baseline estimation | Simple, intuitive rule of thumb |

---

### Pure Term vs Return of Premium (TROP) Economics

Many policyholders are tempted by **Return of Premium (TROP)** plans with the promise of "getting all premiums back if you survive". However, actuarial and investment mathematics reveal that TROP is highly inefficient:

$$\text{TROP Premium} \approx 2.2 \times \text{Pure Term Premium}$$

By selecting **Pure Term** and investing the $1.2\times$ premium difference into an **Equity Index Mutual Fund (SIP @ 12% CAGR)**, a 30-year-old individual generates **₹90 Lakhs to ₹1.5 Crores in extra liquid wealth** over a 35-year policy term compared to nominal TROP refunds.
