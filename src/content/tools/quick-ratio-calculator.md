## Mastering the Quick Ratio (Acid-Test) in Corporate Finance

The Quick Ratio provides an unvarnished view of immediate balance sheet solvency by assessing assets that can be mobilized within hours:

$$\text{Quick Ratio} = \frac{\text{Cash} + \text{Marketable Securities} + \text{Accounts Receivable (Net)}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$$

$$\text{Quick Working Capital} = \text{Quick Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}$$

---

### Liquidity Ratios Hierarchy

| Liquidity Metric | Assets Included | Stringency | Typical Target Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Current Ratio** | All Current Assets (Cash, AR, Inventory, Prepaids) | Standard | **1.50x – 2.00x** |
| **Quick Ratio (Acid-Test)** | **Cash + Securities + Accounts Receivable** | **High** | **1.00x – 1.50x** |
| **Cash Ratio** | Cash &amp; Marketable Securities Only | Maximum | **0.50x – 0.80x** |

---

### Sector Acid-Test Norms

* **SaaS & Technology**: Typically **1.50x – 3.00x** (Minimal or zero inventory)
* **Manufacturing**: Typically **1.00x – 1.40x** (Substantial inventory component)
* **Retail & Supermarkets**: Typically **0.40x – 0.80x** (Heavily reliant on fast inventory turns and cash sales)
