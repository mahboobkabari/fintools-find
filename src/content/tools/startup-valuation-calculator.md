## Mastering Startup Pre-Money Valuation Methodologies

Valuing early-stage and high-growth technology startups requires triangulating between qualitative risk-reduction models and quantitative market multiples:

$$V_{\text{scorecard}} = V_{\text{base}} \times \sum (\text{Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i)$$
$$V_{\text{post}} = V_{\text{pre}} + \text{Investment Raised}$$

### Startup Valuation Framework Comparison

| Methodology | Best Stage | Primary Drivers | Key Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scorecard Method (Payne)** | **Seed / Angel** | Team (30%), Market (25%), Product (15%) | Market-calibrated regional standard |
| **Berkus Method** | **Pre-Seed / Idea** | 5 Risk-Reduction Milestones | No revenue forecasting required |
| **VC Exit Method** | **Series A / Institutional** | Terminal Value, ROI Hurdle, Dilution | Aligned with fund return mathematics |
| **ARR Multiple Method** | **Revenue Scaleup** | Annual Recurring Revenue × Multiple | Direct public & private market comps |

### Institutional Dilution & Valuation Benchmarks

* **Pre-Seed / Angel Round**: Target **10% – 15%** round dilution
* **Seed Round**: Target **15% – 25%** round dilution
* **Series A Growth**: Target **20% – 25%** round dilution
