## Understanding Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate) & Real Estate Valuation

The Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate) is the single most fundamental metric used by real estate investors, property appraisers, and commercial brokers to evaluate the yield and valuation of income-producing real estate.

> **Important Disclosure:** Real estate cap rates, Net Operating Income (NOI), and property valuations are educational estimates based on user inputs. Actual property income, operating expenses, tax treatment, and market values vary by asset class, location, and tenancy terms.

---

### Key Real Estate Yield Metrics Comparison

| Metric | Formula | Deducts Operating Expenses? | Deducts Mortgage Debt Service? | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Gross Rental Yield %** | $\frac{\text{Annual Gross Rent}}{\text{Property Value}} \times 100$ | No | No | Top-line gross income benchmark |
| **Cap Rate %** | $\frac{\text{Net Operating Income (NOI)}}{\text{Property Value}} \times 100$ | **Yes (NOI)** | No | Unleveraged property asset yield & valuation |
| **Cash-on-Cash Return %** | $\frac{\text{Pre-Tax Cash Flow}}{\text{Cash Invested}} \times 100$ | **Yes (NOI)** | **Yes (Debt Service)** | Leveraged cash return on actual cash out of pocket |
| **Operating Expense Ratio (OER)** | $\frac{\text{Total Operating Expenses}}{\text{Effective Gross Income}} \times 100$ | N/A | No | Property operational efficiency benchmark |

### Step-by-Step Worked Example

Assume an investor evaluates a commercial retail unit with the following profile:

1. **Property Value & Gross Income**:
   - Property Value: ₹1,00,000,000 (₹1 Crore)
   - Monthly Gross Rent: ₹65,000 / month
   - Other Annual Income: ₹20,000 / year
   - **Gross Potential Income (GPI)** = (₹65,000 × 12) + ₹20,000 = **₹8,00,000**
   - Vacancy & Loss (5%): ₹40,000
   - **Effective Gross Income (EGI)** = ₹8,00,000 − ₹40,000 = **₹7,60,000**

2. **Operating Expenses & Net Operating Income (NOI)**:
   - Annual Property Tax: ₹60,000
   - Annual Property Insurance: ₹24,000
   - Repairs & Maintenance: ₹45,000
   - Property Management Fee (8% of EGI): ₹60,800
   - HOA / Society Charges & Other OpEx: ₹25,000
   - **Total Annual Operating Expenses (OpEx)** = **₹2,14,800**
   - **Net Operating Income (NOI)** = ₹7,60,000 − ₹2,14,800 = **₹5,45,200**

3. **Cap Rate & Valuation Results**:
   - **Cap Rate %** = $\frac{₹5,45,200}{₹1,00,000,000} \times 100 =$ **5.45%**
   - **Implied Valuation at 7.0% Target Cap Rate** = $\frac{₹5,45,200}{0.07} =$ **₹77,88,571**
   - **Operating Expense Ratio (OER %)** = $\frac{₹2,14,800}{₹7,60,000} \times 100 =$ **28.26%**
   - **Cap Rate Spread (vs 8.5% Mortgage Rate)** = 5.45% − 8.50% = **−3.05%** (Negative leverage alert)
