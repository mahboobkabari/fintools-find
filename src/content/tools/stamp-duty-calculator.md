## Understanding Property Stamp Duty & Registration Charges in India

When purchasing residential or commercial real estate in India, statutory government overheads—primarily **Stamp Duty** and **Registration Charges**—represent the single largest upfront cash outflow beyond the property purchase consideration itself. These charges typically add **5% to 8%** to the total acquisition cost and must be paid in cash before the sale deed can be legally executed.

### Income Tax Deductions Under Section 80C

Under **Section 80C** of the Income Tax Act, 1961:
- Homebuyers can claim the actual amount paid towards **Stamp Duty and Registration Charges** as a deduction up to the overall Section 80C limit of **₹1,50,000**.
- The deduction can only be claimed in the **financial year in which the actual payment is made**.
- For an individual in the **30% tax slab** (plus 4% cess), claiming the full ₹1.5 Lakhs deduction results in direct income tax savings of **₹46,800**.
