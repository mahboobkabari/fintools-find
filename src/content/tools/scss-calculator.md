## Understanding Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)

The **Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)** is one of the premier government-backed small savings schemes in India, specifically designed to provide regular, risk-free quarterly income to retirees and senior citizens. Governed by the **Ministry of Finance Senior Citizens Savings Scheme Rules 2019**, SCSS offers an attractive interest rate of **8.2% per annum** (notified quarterly), which is significantly higher than standard bank fixed deposits.

Key benefits of SCSS include:
1. **100% Capital Safety**: Backed directly by the Government of India (sovereign guarantee).
2. **Guaranteed Quarterly Passive Income**: Interest is calculated at the annual rate divided by 4 and credited on the first working day of April, July, October, and January.
3. **Statutory Deposit Limits**: Up to ₹30 Lakhs per individual, and up to ₹60 Lakhs combined for eligible senior couples.
4. **Tax Deduction under Section 80C**: Up to ₹1.5 Lakhs eligible for tax deduction in the financial year of deposit (under Old Tax Regime).
5. **Section 80TTB Tax Relief**: Senior citizens can claim up to ₹50,000 tax-free interest deduction per financial year.

## SCSS Eligibility Criteria

To open an SCSS account, an applicant must satisfy any of the following statutory eligibility categories:

| Category | Minimum Age Requirement | Conditions & Documents Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Senior Citizens** | Age 60 years or above | Proof of age (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID) |
| **Retired Civilian Employees** | Age 55 to 60 years | Must retire on superannuation or VRS; deposit within 1 month of receiving retirement benefits |
| **Retired Defense Personnel** | Age 50 years or above | Defense service retirees (excluding defense civilian); deposit within 1 month of receiving retirement benefits |

## SCSS Statutory Deposit Limits & Account Categories

Under Budget 2023 notifications (effective April 1, 2023), the individual deposit cap was doubled from ₹15 Lakhs to ₹30 Lakhs.

- **Individual Account Limit**: Maximum **₹30,000,000 (₹30 Lakhs)**. Deposits must be made in multiples of ₹1,000.
- **Joint Account with Spouse**: A depositor can open a joint account with their spouse. In a joint account, the entire deposit belongs to the primary holder. However, the spouse can also open an independent account or joint account as primary holder for up to another ₹30 Lakhs, allowing an eligible couple to invest up to **₹60,000,000 (₹60 Lakhs)** total.

## SCSS Interest Calculation & Payout Schedule

SCSS interest is computed as a simple interest payout quarterly:

$$\text{Quarterly Interest Payout} = \text{Deposit Amount} \times \left( \frac{\text{Annual Interest Rate} / 100}{4} \right)$$

For example, on a maximum individual deposit of **₹30,000,000 (₹30 Lakhs)** at **8.2% p.a.**:

$$\text{Quarterly Income} = 3,000,000 \times \left( \frac{0.082}{4} \right) = ₹61,500 \text{ per quarter}$$

### Calendar Quarterly Payout Schedule:
- **Q1 Payout**: April 1st
- **Q2 Payout**: July 1st
- **Q3 Payout**: October 1st
- **Q4 Payout**: January 1st

## Tax Rules: Section 80C, Section 80TTB & Section 194A TDS

SCSS interest income is taxable at your applicable marginal income tax slab rate. However, specific tax provisions apply:

### 1. Section 80C Upfront Tax Deduction
- The principal amount deposited in an SCSS account qualifies for tax deduction under **Section 80C** of the Income Tax Act, up to a maximum limit of **₹1,50,000** per financial year (under Old Tax Regime).

### 2. Section 80TTB Senior Citizen Interest Exemption
- Under **Section 80TTB**, senior citizens (aged 60 and above) can claim a deduction of up to **₹50,000** on interest earned from bank and post office deposits, including SCSS.
- Only interest income exceeding ₹50,000 per financial year is included in taxable income.

### 3. Section 194A TDS Rules & Form 15H
- Under **Section 194A**, Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) applies if total SCSS interest paid in a financial year exceeds **₹50,000** for senior citizens.
- **With Valid PAN**: TDS is deducted at **10%**.
- **Without PAN (Section 206AA)**: TDS is deducted at **20%**.
- **Form 15H Submission**: Senior citizens whose total estimated tax liability for the financial year is **NIL** can submit **Form 15H** at the post office or bank branch to receive 100% full gross quarterly interest payouts without any TDS deduction.

## SCSS Premature Closure Rules & Penalty Tiers

While SCSS has a statutory tenure of **5 years**, premature withdrawal or account closure is permitted subject to specific penalty deductions:

| Exit Timing Horizon | Premature Closure Penalty Tier | Net Impact on Principal / Interest |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exit Before 1 Year** | 100% Interest Recovery | **No interest is payable.** Any quarterly interest already paid will be recovered by deducting it from the principal amount refunded. |
| **Exit Between 1Y and 2Y** | **1.5% Principal Deduction** | 1.5% of the principal deposit is deducted as penalty. The remaining 98.5% principal is refunded. |
| **Exit Between 2Y and 5Y** | **1.0% Principal Deduction** | 1.0% of the principal deposit is deducted as penalty. The remaining 99.0% principal is refunded. |
| **Full 5-Year Maturity** | **0% Penalty** | 100% principal is refunded, and all 20 quarterly interest payouts are retained. |

---

## SCSS Extension After Maturity

Upon completing the 5-year maturity period, account holders have the option to:
1. **Close the Account**: Receive full principal refund.
2. **Extend for 3 Years**: Submit an extension request (Form 4) within 1 year of maturity. The extended account earns interest at the rate applicable on the date of maturity.
3. **Premature Exit during Extension**: The extended account can be closed anytime after 1 year of extension without paying any premature closure penalty.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How is SCSS interest calculated?
SCSS interest is calculated using simple interest formula: $\text{Quarterly Interest} = \text{Deposit} \times (r / 400)$. It does not compound quarter-on-quarter because interest is paid out directly into the depositor's savings bank account every quarter.

### 2. Can I open multiple SCSS accounts?
Yes, an eligible individual can open multiple SCSS accounts across different post offices or authorized commercial banks, provided the aggregate principal balance across all accounts does not exceed the statutory limit of ₹30 Lakhs.

### 3. Is Form 15H mandatory for SCSS?
Form 15H is not mandatory, but highly recommended for senior citizens whose total annual taxable income is below the basic exemption threshold. Submitting Form 15H prevents 10% TDS deduction under Section 194A on interest payouts exceeding ₹50,000.

### 4. What happens to the SCSS account in the event of the depositor's death?
In the event of the account holder's death, the account earns interest at the post office savings account rate (currently 4.0% p.a.) from the date of death until the account is closed and settled with the nominee or legal heir.
