# Personal Net Worth Calculator: Comprehensive Balance Sheet & Wealth Engine

Calculating your **personal net worth** is the ultimate scorecard for measuring long-term financial progress. Rather than focusing solely on income, tracking your assets against your liabilities reveals your true equity, debt burden, and financial resilience.

---

## 1. The Fundamental Net Worth Accounting Identity

Net worth is governed by the core balance-sheet identity:

$$\text{Net Worth} = \sum \text{Total Assets} - \sum \text{Total Liabilities}$$

- **Gross Assets**: Everything of economic value that you own, categorized by liquidity:
  - **Liquid Cash & Cash Equivalents**: Checking accounts, high-yield savings accounts, certificates of deposit (CDs), and emergency reserves.
  - **Investable Portfolios**: Public equities, index mutual funds, ETFs, government/corporate bonds, and cryptocurrency.
  - **Retirement Accounts**: 401(k), Traditional/Roth IRAs, EPF, PPF, and Superannuation funds.
  - **Real Estate & Tangible Property**: Primary residences, rental properties, land, vehicles, and precious metals.

- **Total Liabilities**: Everything you legally owe to financial institutions or creditors:
  - **Short-Term Debt**: Credit card revolving balances, medical bills, and personal unsecured loans.
  - **Long-Term Debt**: Residential mortgages, auto loans, student loans, and business financing.

---

## 2. Total Net Worth vs. Liquid Net Worth vs. Investable Net Worth

Evaluating your balance sheet through multiple lenses provides critical clarity:

```
Gross Assets ($800k)
   ├── Liquid Assets ($150k) ───────────► Cash, Stocks, ETFs
   ├── Investable Retirement ($250k) ───► 401(k), IRAs, EPF
   └── Illiquid Assets ($400k) ─────────► Primary Home, Vehicles
            │
            ▼
Total Liabilities ($350k)
   ├── Short-Term Debt ($10k) ──────────► Credit Cards, Personal Loans
   └── Long-Term Debt ($340k) ──────────► Home Mortgage, Car Loan
            │
            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Total Net Worth       = $800k - $350k = $450,000      │
│ Liquid Net Worth      = $150k - $10k  = $140,000      │
│ Investable Net Worth  = $400k - $10k  = $390,000      │
│ Home Equity           = $350k - $320k = $30,000       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Liquid Net Worth
Liquid Net Worth measures your immediate solvency by subtracting short-term liabilities from cash and marketable securities. Having a positive liquid net worth ensures you can weather job loss or market downturns without being forced to liquidate illiquid real estate at distressed prices.

### Investable Net Worth
Investable Net Worth isolates income-generating investment assets from personal-use property. In the FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) movement, the 4% safe withdrawal rule applies specifically to your investable net worth rather than your primary residence.

## 3. Key Financial Ratios to Benchmark

| Metric | Calculation Formula | Healthy Target Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Debt-to-Asset Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Total Liabilities}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times 100$ | $< 30\%$ (Low Risk) · $30\% - 50\%$ (Moderate) |
| **Net Worth to Assets** | $\frac{\text{Net Worth}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times 100$ | $> 60\%$ Equity Retention |
| **Emergency Cash Runway** | $\frac{\text{Pure Cash Reserves}}{\text{Monthly Essential Expenses}}$ | $3 \text{ to } 6 \text{ Months of Expenses}$ |
| **Liquid Asset Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Liquid Assets}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times 100$ | $> 20\%$ for working professionals |

## 4. Identifying Wealth Concentration Risks

A well-constructed balance sheet minimizes catastrophic downside through diversification. Our calculator automatically evaluates five concentration warning triggers:
1. **Real Estate Concentration ($> 60\%$)**: Having too much wealth tied up in illiquid property leaves you vulnerable to localized housing market downturns and property tax increases.
2. **Cryptocurrency Exposure ($> 15\%$)**: High digital asset volatility can cause extreme balance sheet swings.
3. **Cash Drag ($> 35\%$)**: Holding excessive uninvested cash over long periods erodes purchasing power due to inflation.
4. **Elevated Leverage ($> 50\%$)**: When debt finances more than half of your assets, small asset price declines can push net worth into negative territory.
5. **Single Asset Concentration ($> 40\%$)**: Over-relying on a single stock or private business equity increases idiosyncratic risk.

---

## 5. Tracking Net Worth Velocity (CAGR)

Tracking your balance sheet quarterly or annually reveals your wealth growth trajectory:

$$\text{CAGR} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Worth}_{\text{Current}}}{\text{Net Worth}_{\text{Initial}}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\text{Years}}} - 1$$

Consistently achieving a 10% to 15% annual compound growth rate across your 20s, 30s, and 40s compounds modest early savings into substantial generational wealth.
