## Understanding Personal Net Worth Accounting

Your personal net worth is the ultimate financial scoreboard. Unlike gross salary or monthly income, net worth reflects your true accumulated wealth by subtracting all outstanding debts from your gross assets.

---

### Net Worth & Financial Position Matrix

| Financial Metric | Formula | Financial Meaning | Target Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Net Worth** | $\text{Total Assets} - \text{Total Liabilities}$ | Net financial equity owned free of debt | Positive & growing year-over-year |
| **Net Worth / Assets** | $\frac{\text{Net Worth}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times 100$ | Percentage of assets unencumbered by debt | $> 50\%$ (Healthy equity position) |
| **Debt-to-Asset Ratio** | $\frac{\text{Total Liabilities}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times 100$ | Proportion of total assets funded by debt | $< 50\%$ (Lower borrowing risk) |
| **Liquidity Coverage** | $\frac{\text{Liquid Assets}}{\text{Monthly Essential Expenses}}$ | Months of living expenses covered by cash/stocks | 3 to 6 Months minimum buffer |

### Step-by-Step Worked Example

Assume an individual has the following financial profile:

1. **Assets**:
   - Savings Account & Cash: ₹2,00,000
   - Stock & Mutual Fund SIPs: ₹6,00,000
   - EPF & PPF Balance: ₹8,00,000
   - Primary Residence (Apartment): ₹50,00,000
   - **Total Assets** = ₹66,00,000

2. **Liabilities**:
   - Credit Card Balance: ₹30,000
   - Outstanding Home Loan: ₹22,00,000
   - **Total Liabilities** = ₹22,30,000

3. **Net Worth Calculation**:
   $$\text{Net Worth} = ₹66,00,000 - ₹22,30,000 = ₹43,70,000$$

4. **Health Metrics**:
   - **Net Worth / Assets**: $\frac{43.7}{66.0} \times 100 = 66.2\%$
   - **Debt-to-Asset Ratio**: $\frac{22.3}{66.0} \times 100 = 33.8\%$
   - **Liquid Assets**: ₹2,00,000 + ₹6,00,000 = ₹8,00,000
