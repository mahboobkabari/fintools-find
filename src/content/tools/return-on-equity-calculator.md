## Mastering Return on Equity (ROE) & DuPont Analysis

Return on Equity measures corporate management's capital allocation efficiency:

$$\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shareholders' Equity}} \times 100$$

$$\text{DuPont ROE} = \text{Net Profit Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}$$

| **Retail Supermarkets** | High Asset Turnover | Thin (2%–4%) | High (2.5x–3.5x) | Moderate (2.0x) | **18% – 25%** |
| **Commercial Banking** | Financial Leverage | Moderate | Low | High (6.0x–10.0x) | **14% – 18%** |
| **Infrastructure / Utilities** | Capital-Intensive Assets | Moderate | Low (0.4x–0.6x) | Moderate (2.0x–3.0x) | **9% – 12%** |
