## What is a Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) Calculator?

A **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) Calculator** is an institutional financial planning tool designed to project the future maturity corpus and tax savings accumulated under the Government of India's Sukanya Samriddhi Account Scheme 2019. It helps parents calculate:

1. **100% Tax-Free Maturity Corpus**: The final lump sum accumulated at 21 years from account opening at the official **8.2% p.a.** government-notified interest rate.
2. **15-Year Contribution Schedule**: Year-by-year tracking of annual deposits made during the statutory 15-year contribution window.
3. **Section 80C & EEE Tax Savings**: Annual and cumulative income tax savings achieved under Section 80C and Section 10(11A).
4. **50% Higher Education Admission Withdrawal**: Simulating partial balance withdrawal when the girl child turns age 18 to pay for college fees.
5. **Guaranteed SSY vs. Market Equity SIP**: Comparing sovereign government-backed returns against market-linked equity mutual fund growth.

## Statutory Scheme Rules & Compounding Math

### 1. Annual Deposit Compounding (March 31st Crediting)
$$\text{Years 1 to 15 (Contribution Phase): } A_y = (A_{y-1} + \text{Deposit}_y) \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{Years 16 to 21 (Non-Contribution Compounding): } A_y = A_{y-1} \times \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)$$

Where:
* $\text{Deposit}_y$ = Annual contribution (Min ₹250, Max ₹150,000) for Years $1 \le y \le 15$
* $r$ = Notified interest rate (% p.a., default 8.2%)
* $A_y$ = Account balance at end of Year $y$

### 2. Section 80C & Section 10(11A) EEE Tax Audit
$$\text{Annual Tax Saved} = \text{Deposit} \times \frac{\text{MarginalTaxBracket}}{100}$$
$$\text{TDS Deducted} = ₹0 \quad (\text{100\% Tax-Free under Sec 10(11A)})$$
