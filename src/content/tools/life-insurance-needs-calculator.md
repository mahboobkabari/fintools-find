## Understanding Term Life Insurance Needs Analysis

Securing term life insurance is the foundational cornerstone of personal financial planning. Rather than relying on arbitrary rules of thumb or aggressive sales pitches, a structured needs-based calculation evaluates your exact balance sheet obligations and liquid wealth to protect your family's future.

---

### DIME Protection Framework Matrix

| Needs Component | Financial Definition | Calculation Basis | Planning Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Debt & Final Expenses** | Outstanding non-mortgage debts & final administrative costs | Immediate lump-sum settlement sum | Zero debt burden on surviving family |
| **Income Replacement** | Present value of annual take-home income over dependency years | Inflation-adjusted PV over 10–20 years | Maintain family living standard |
| **Mortgage Payoff** | Total outstanding principal on primary home loan | Immediate home loan clearance | Retain family home ownership |
| **Education & Milestones** | Future children higher education & marriage funds | Inflation-adjusted future goal present value | Fund milestone life goals |

---

### Step-by-Step Worked Example

Assume a 32-year-old earner has the following family financial profile:

1. **Financial Obligations**:
   - Outstanding Mortgage / Home Loan: ₹40,00,000
   - Personal Loan Debts: ₹5,00,000
   - Children Education Fund (Future): ₹20,00,000
   - Final Administrative Expenses: ₹2,00,000
   - **Total Debt & Goal Obligations** = ₹67,00,000

2. **Income Replacement PV**:
   - Annual Income: ₹12,00,000
   - Replacement Period: 15 Years (at 5% salary growth & 6% discount rate)
   - **Income Replacement PV** = ₹1,37,60,000

3. **Total Gross Need**:
   $$\text{Gross Need} = ₹67,00,000 + ₹1,37,60,000 = ₹2,04,60,000$$

4. **Existing Available Resources**:
   - Active Term Life Policy: ₹50,00,000
   - Bank Savings & Cash: ₹6,00,000
   - Investments & EPF: ₹14,00,000
   - **Total Existing Resources** = ₹70,00,000

5. **Estimated Additional Coverage Needed**:
   $$\text{Net Cover Needed} = ₹2,04,60,000 - ₹70,00,000 = ₹1,34,60,000$$
