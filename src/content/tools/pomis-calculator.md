## Understanding Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS 2019)

The **Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS)** is a flagship small savings instrument administered by **India Post (Ministry of Communications)** and notified by the **Ministry of Finance, Government of India**. It is tailored for retirees, senior citizens, and risk-averse depositors seeking a guaranteed monthly income stream with 100% sovereign safety.

---

## Key Statutory Features of POMIS

| Feature Parameter | Statutory Provision & Scheme Rules |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Current Interest Rate** | **7.4% p.a.** (notified quarterly by Ministry of Finance) |
| **Payout Frequency** | **Monthly Payout** (credited on the same date each month) |
| **Tenure / Maturity Horizon** | **Exactly 5 Years** (60 Months) |
| **Single Account Maximum Deposit** | **₹9,00,000** (₹9 Lakhs) |
| **Joint Account Maximum Deposit** | **₹15,00,000** (₹15 Lakhs for up to 3 joint holders) |
| **Minimum Deposit** | **₹1,000** (in multiples of ₹1,000) |
| **Sovereign Guarantee** | **100% Government of India Backed** (Zero default risk) |
| **TDS at Source** | **No TDS deducted** by Post Office on monthly payouts |

---

## Monthly Interest Calculation & Deposit Limits

$$\text{Monthly Interest Payout} = \frac{\text{Deposit Amount} \times 7.4\%}{12}$$

### Statutory Deposit Limits:
- **Single Account (Max ₹9 Lakhs)**: Generates **₹5,550 per month** (₹66,600 per year / ₹3,33,000 total 5-year interest).
- **Joint Account (Max ₹15 Lakhs)**: Generates **₹9,250 per month** (₹1,11,000 per year / ₹5,55,000 total 5-year interest).

> **Note on Joint Accounts**: All joint account holders have an equal share in the deposit. An individual's share in joint accounts is added to their single account deposits to enforce the personal statutory limit of ₹9 Lakhs per individual across India.

## Premature Account Closure Rules & Penalties

POMIS accounts have a 5-year lock-in, but statutory premature withdrawal is permitted after 1 year subject to principal deductions:

| Account Duration Period | Statutory Premature Closure Rule & Penalty |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0 to 1 Year** | **Closure NOT permitted** under statutory rules. |
| **1 Year to 3 Years** | Account can be closed after deducting **2% penalty** from principal deposit. |
| **3 Years to 5 Years** | Account can be closed after deducting **1% penalty** from principal deposit. |

## Guaranteed Sovereign POMIS vs Bank Fixed Deposit

Depositors evaluating 5-year monthly payout options frequently compare POMIS against 5-Year Commercial Bank Fixed Deposits:

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How is monthly interest paid out in POMIS?
Monthly interest is credited automatically into the depositor's Post Office Savings Account (POSA) or auto-transferred via ECS/NEFT into a linked commercial bank savings account.

### 2. What happens if monthly interest is not withdrawn?
If the monthly interest is not withdrawn by the depositor, it remains in the account but **does NOT earn any additional interest**. Unclaimed monthly interest earns zero compound interest.

### 3. Is Section 80C tax deduction available on POMIS deposits?
No. Investments into POMIS do NOT qualify for Section 80C tax deductions under the Income Tax Act.
