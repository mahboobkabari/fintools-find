## Understanding National Savings Certificate (NSC VIII Issue)

The **National Savings Certificate (NSC)** is one of India's premier government-backed small savings deposit schemes. Administered by **India Post (Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications)** and notified by the **Ministry of Finance**, NSC offers individual investors 100% sovereign security, attractive fixed interest rates, and powerful Section 80C tax benefits.

## Key Statutory Features of NSC (VIII Issue)

| Feature Parameter | Statutory Provision & Government Notification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Current Interest Rate** | **7.7% p.a.** (notified quarterly by Ministry of Finance) |
| **Compounding Frequency** | **Compounded Annually**, paid as a lump sum at maturity |
| **Tenure / Maturity Period** | **Exactly 5 Years** (60 Months) |
| **Minimum Investment** | **₹1,000** (in multiples of ₹100) |
| **Maximum Upper Limit** | **No Maximum Limit** (Unlimited investment permitted) |
| **Sovereign Capital Safety** | **100% Government of India Backed** (Zero default risk) |
| **TDS at Maturity** | **No Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** by Post Office |

## Income Tax Section 80C & Deemed Reinvestment Rules

NSC is uniquely structured under the **Income Tax Act, 1961** to provide tax benefits across multiple financial years:

```
[Year 1 Deposit] ────► Sec 80C Upfront Tax Deduction (Up to ₹1,50,000)
[Year 1 Interest] ───► Deemed Reinvested ───► Sec 80C Deduction (FY 1)
[Year 2 Interest] ───► Deemed Reinvested ───► Sec 80C Deduction (FY 2)
[Year 3 Interest] ───► Deemed Reinvested ───► Sec 80C Deduction (FY 3)
[Year 4 Interest] ───► Deemed Reinvested ───► Sec 80C Deduction (FY 4)
[Year 5 Interest] ───► Paid at Maturity  ───► Fully Taxable at Marginal Slab
```

### Breakdown of Tax Provisions:
1. **Year 1 Initial Principal**: Your initial deposit up to ₹1.5 Lakhs is eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C.
2. **Years 1 to 4 Accrued Interest**: The interest accrued at the end of Years 1, 2, 3, and 4 is not paid out; it is automatically added back to the deposit. Under tax law, this accrued interest is **deemed to be reinvested** in NSC and qualifies for Section 80C deduction in each respective financial year.
3. **Year 5 Maturity Interest**: The interest accrued in the 5th (final) year cannot be reinvested because the certificate matures. Therefore, Year 5 interest is fully taxable under "Income from Other Sources" at your applicable tax slab rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How is NSC maturity calculated?
NSC maturity is calculated using the annual compounding formula: $A = P \times (1 + r/100)^5$, where $r = 7.7\%$ p.a.

### 2. Can NSC be transferred or pledged as collateral?
Yes. NSC certificates can be pledged or transferred as security to banks and government institutions for securing business or personal loans.

### 3. Can a joint account or minor account be opened?
Yes. NSC accounts can be opened individually, jointly by up to 3 adults, or by a guardian on behalf of a minor child.
