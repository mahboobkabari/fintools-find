## Understanding Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

**Atal Pension Yojana (APY)** is a premier government-backed social security pension scheme launched by the **Government of India** and administered by the **Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)**. It is specifically designed to provide financial security and guaranteed monthly income during retirement for citizens in the unorganized sector.

---

## Key Statutory Features of APY

| Feature Parameter | Statutory Provision & Scheme Rules |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eligibility Window** | **Indian Citizens Aged 18 to 40 Years** |
| **Vesting Age** | **60 Years** (Pension Starts Automatically) |
| **Guaranteed Monthly Pension** | **₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 / month** |
| **Auto-Debit Frequencies** | **Monthly, Quarterly, or Half-Yearly** |
| **Sovereign Guarantee** | **100% Government of India Guarantee** |
| **Spouse Pension** | **Same Guaranteed Monthly Pension** paid to spouse upon subscriber death |
| **Nominee Corpus Return** | **100% Return of Corpus** (up to ₹8.5 Lakhs) to nominee |
| **Tax-Payer Restriction** | **Not Eligible for New Enrolment** if income tax payer (post Oct 1, 2022) |

## Nominee Corpus Return Schedule

Upon the death of both the subscriber and spouse, PFRDA returns 100% of the accumulated pension corpus to the nominee:

- **₹1,000 / Month Pension Tier**: **₹1,70,000** returned to nominee
- **₹2,000 / Month Pension Tier**: **₹3,40,000** returned to nominee
- **₹3,000 / Month Pension Tier**: **₹5,10,000** returned to nominee
- **₹4,000 / Month Pension Tier**: **₹6,80,000** returned to nominee
- **₹5,000 / Month Pension Tier**: **₹8,50,000** returned to nominee

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What happens if I miss an auto-debit contribution payment?
If auto-debit fails due to insufficient balance, banks charge a small monthly penalty (₹1/month for contributions up to ₹100, ₹2/month for contributions ₹101-₹500, ₹5/month for ₹501-₹1000, and ₹10/month for above ₹1000).

### 2. Is Atal Pension Yojana (APY) guaranteed by the Government of India?
Yes. APY features an explicit sovereign guarantee by the Government of India through PFRDA. If the actual investment returns are lower than required, the government co-funds the shortfall.

### 3. Can income tax payers join APY?
No. Effective October 1, 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer is not eligible to open a new APY account.
