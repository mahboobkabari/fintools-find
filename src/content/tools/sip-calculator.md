## What is a SIP Calculator?

A **SIP Calculator** (Systematic Investment Plan Calculator) is a financial planning tool that estimates the future wealth corpus generated by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals into equity or debt mutual funds.

Unlike traditional lump-sum deposits, systematic investing allows individual investors to build long-term wealth progressively from monthly income while reducing market volatility through **Rupee Cost Averaging**.

> **Definition (Featured Snippet):**  
> A **Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)** is an investment strategy where an investor commits a fixed sum at recurring intervals into mutual funds. It leverages monthly compounding interest and Rupee Cost Averaging to build long-term capital wealth.

---

## The SIP Calculation Formula

Mutual fund SIP returns are calculated using the standard **Future Value of Annuity Due Formula**:

$$\text{FV} = P \times \left[ \frac{(1+i)^n - 1}{i} \right] \times (1+i)$$

### Variable Definitions
* **$\text{FV}$ (Future Value):** The total maturity wealth corpus accumulated at the end of the investment tenure.
* **$P$ (Monthly Contribution):** The fixed amount invested each month.
* **$i$ (Monthly Interest Rate):** The expected annual return rate divided by 12 months and expressed as a decimal:
  $$i = \frac{\text{Annual Expected Return \%}}{12 \times 100}$$
* **$n$ (Total Months):** The duration of the investment in months ($\text{Tenure in Years} \times 12$).

## Practical Worked Examples

### Example 1: Wealth Accumulation (₹5,000/Month @ 12% for 10 Years)

Suppose you invest **₹5,000 per month** in an equity mutual fund scheme expecting an average **12% annual return** over **10 years** (120 months):

1. **Total Invested Amount:** $₹5,000 \times 120 = \mathbf{₹6,00,000}$
2. **Monthly Return Rate ($i$):** $(12 / 12) / 100 = 0.01$
3. **Maturity Wealth Corpus ($\text{FV}$):** **₹11,61,695**
4. **Estimated Wealth Gain:** $₹11,61,695 - ₹6,00,000 = \mathbf{₹5,61,695}$

Notice that in 10 years at 12% p.a., your wealth gain (₹5.61 Lakhs) almost matches your total out-of-pocket investment (₹6 Lakhs).

---

### Example 2: Long-Term Compounding Power (₹10,000/Month @ 12% for 20 Years)

If you double the tenure to **20 years** (240 months) with **₹10,00,000** monthly investment:

1. **Total Invested Amount:** $₹10,000 \times 240 = \mathbf{₹24,00,000}$
2. **Maturity Wealth Corpus ($\text{FV}$):** **₹99,91,479** (~₹1 Crore)
3. **Estimated Wealth Gain:** $₹99,91,479 - ₹24,00,000 = \mathbf{₹75,91,479}$

Over 20 years, **76% of your final net worth consists of pure compound interest gains**, demonstrating why compounding rewards long-term investors.

## SIP vs. Lumpsum Investment: Key Comparison

| Feature | Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) | Lumpsum Investment |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital Requirement** | Small regular amounts (from ₹500/month). | Large one-time upfront capital. |
| **Market Risk** | Lower (Rupee Cost Averaging offsets market drops). | Higher (Timing the market peak can reduce short-term returns). |
| **Investor Discipline** | High (Automated monthly bank debits). | Requires manual execution and capital availability. |
| **Best Used For** | Salaried employees & regular income earners. | Windfall gains, annual bonuses, or inheritance funds. |
