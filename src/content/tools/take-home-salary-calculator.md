## What is a Take-home Salary Calculator?

A **Take-home Salary Calculator** (also known as a **Net Salary Calculator** or **In-Hand Pay Estimator**) converts annual gross Cost to Company (CTC) into exact monthly bank deposits for **Financial Year 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)**.

When you receive a new job offer letter or annual performance appraisal, headline CTC figures include mandatory deductions like Employee Provident Fund (EPF), Professional Tax (PT), and Income Tax TDS. Calculating your exact monthly in-hand pay enables accurate budget planning, rental choices, and EMI management.

### Who Should Use It & When?
* **Job Seekers & Interviewees:** When evaluating offer letters and comparing competing corporate compensation packages.
* **Salaried Employees:** At annual appraisal time to calculate net monthly salary increments.
* **Home & Car Buyers:** To calculate affordable monthly EMI payments using our [EMI Calculator](/tools/loans/emi-calculator/) based on real net cash flow.
* **Taxpayers Planning Budgeting:** To understand the exact impact of the ₹75,000 standard deduction under the **New Tax Regime (FY 2025-26)**.

## Breakdown Components of Annual CTC

Your gross annual CTC is split into gross salary allowances and statutory deductions:

```
Gross Annual CTC
├── Employer EPF Contribution (12% Basic, if included in CTC)
├── Gross Annual Salary
│   ├── Basic Salary (Typically 40% to 50% of CTC)
│   ├── House Rent Allowance (HRA) (Exemptible under Old Regime via HRA Calculator)
│   └── Special / Conveyance Allowances
└── Employee Statutory Deductions & Taxes
    ├── Income Tax (CBDT Slabs minus ₹75,000 Standard Deduction)
    ├── Employee Provident Fund (EPF - 12% of Basic)
    └── Professional Tax (PT - Max ₹2,400/year)
```

## Take-Home Salary Calculation Formulas

### 1. Gross Annual Salary Formula
$$\text{Gross Annual Salary} = \text{Annual CTC} - \text{Employer EPF Contribution}$$

### 2. Taxable Income Formula (FY 2025-26 New Tax Regime)
$$\text{Taxable Income} = \max(0, \text{Gross Salary} - \text{₹75,000 Standard Deduction})$$

### 3. Net Annual Take-Home Formula
$$\text{Total Deductions} = \text{Income Tax Payable} + \text{Annual Employee EPF (12\% Basic)} + \text{Professional Tax}$$

$$\text{Net Annual Take-Home} = \text{Gross Salary} - \text{Total Deductions}$$

### 4. Net Monthly In-Hand Pay Formula
$$\text{Net Monthly Take-Home} = \frac{\text{Net Annual Take-Home}}{12}$$

## Practical Worked Examples (FY 2025-26 Tax Rules)

### Example 1: Benchmark ₹12 Lakh CTC Package (50% Basic, Employer EPF Included)

Suppose an employee receives a **CTC offer of ₹12,00,000 (₹12 Lakhs)** with **50% Basic Salary (₹6,00,000)** and **₹2,400 Professional Tax** under the **New Tax Regime**:

1. **Annual CTC:** **₹12,00,000**
2. **Employer EPF (12% of ₹6L Basic):** **₹72,000**
3. **Gross Annual Salary:** $₹12,00,000 - ₹72,000 = \mathbf{₹11,28,000}$
4. **Standard Deduction (New Regime):** **₹75,000**
5. **Net Taxable Income:** $₹11,28,000 - ₹75,000 = \mathbf{₹10,53,000}$
6. **Income Tax Payable (New Regime + 4% Cess):** **₹71,500**
7. **Employee EPF Deduction (12% of ₹6L Basic):** **₹72,000** (₹6,000/month)
8. **Annual Professional Tax:** **₹2,400** (₹200/month)
9. **Total Annual Employee Deductions:** $₹71,500 + ₹72,000 + ₹2,400 = \mathbf{₹1,45,900}$
10. **Net Annual In-Hand Salary:** $₹11,28,000 - ₹1,45,900 = \mathbf{₹9,82,100}$
11. **Net Monthly Bank Deposit:** $\frac{₹9,82,100}{12} = \mathbf{₹81,842\text{ per month}}$ (if employer EPF in CTC) / **₹87,842 per month** (if employer EPF separate).

### Example 2: ₹7.75 Lakh CTC Package (Zero Income Tax Scenario)

Suppose an employee earns **₹7,75,000 CTC** (Employer EPF separate) with **₹3,87,500 Basic Salary**:

1. **Gross Salary:** **₹7,75,000**
2. **Standard Deduction:** **₹75,000**
3. **Net Taxable Income:** $₹7,75,000 - ₹75,000 = \mathbf{₹7,00,000}$
4. **Income Tax Payable:** **₹0** (Section 87A 100% Tax Rebate applies since taxable income $\le$ ₹7L)
5. **Annual Employee EPF (12% of Basic):** **₹46,500**
6. **Professional Tax:** **₹2,400**
7. **Net Annual Take-Home:** $₹7,75,000 - ₹48,900 = \mathbf{₹7,26,100}$
8. **Net Monthly Bank Deposit:** $\frac{₹7,26,100}{12} = \mathbf{₹60,508\text{ per month}}$
