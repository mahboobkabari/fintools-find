## Mastering SaaS Recurring Revenue & Waterfall Dynamics

For subscription software companies, B2B platforms, and membership businesses, **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** and **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)** represent the core indicators of compound scalability:

$$\text{Net New MRR} = (\text{New MRR} + \text{Expansion MRR} + \text{Reactivation MRR}) - (\text{Contraction MRR} + \text{Churned MRR})$$
$$\text{Run-Rate ARR} = \text{Ending MRR} \times 12$$

### Enterprise Valuation Multiple Impact

Adding incremental Net New MRR creates outsized shareholder enterprise value when capitalized through ARR multiples:

$$\text{Implied Valuation} = (\text{Ending MRR} \times 12) \times \text{Valuation Multiple}$$

For example, adding **₹1,00,000 in Net New MRR** increases Run-Rate ARR by **₹12 Lakhs**, which at a **10x ARR Multiple** creates an immediate **₹1.20 Crores increase in enterprise value**.
