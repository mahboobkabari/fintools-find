## Understanding Cap Table Mechanics & Option Pool Shuffles

When raising venture capital, startup founders issue newly authorized equity in exchange for investment capital:

$$\text{Post-Money Valuation} = \text{Pre-Money Valuation} + \text{Investment Raised}$$
$$\text{Investor Ownership \%} = \frac{\text{Investment Raised}}{\text{Post-Money Valuation}} \times 100$$

### Pre-Money vs Post-Money Option Pool Comparison

| Structure | Who Absorbs ESOP Dilution | Effective Founder Dilution | Term Sheet Friendliness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-Money Option Pool (VC Shuffle)** | **Founders Only (100%)** | **Higher (e.g. 30%)** | **Standard VC Term Sheet** |
| **Post-Money Option Pool** | **Founders & Investors Pro-Rata** | **Lower (e.g. 27%)** | **Founder-Friendly** |
| **Zero Pool Expansion** | **None** | **Lowest (e.g. 20%)** | **Rare in Early-Stage** |

### Multi-Round Dilution Waterfall Target Benchmarks

Founders must plan their equity trajectory across subsequent funding rounds to retain control, alignment, and economic upside:

* **Pre-Seed / Angel**: Founders retain **80% – 90%**
* **Seed Round**: Founders retain **65% – 75%**
* **Series A**: Founders retain **45% – 55%**
* **Series B**: Founders retain **30% – 40%**
* **Exit / IPO**: Founders retain **15% – 25%**
