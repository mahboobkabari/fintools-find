## Determining Family Health Insurance Sum Insured & Tax Savings

Hospitalization expenses in urban centers double approximately every 5 to 6 years due to 12-15% annual medical inflation.

> **Educational Scenario Disclaimer:** This calculator provides illustrative scenario modeling based on user-entered healthcare parameters. Premium estimates are benchmarks and do not constitute insurance company quotes or underwriting guarantees.

---

### Key Health Insurance Policy Terms Comparison

| Feature | Description | Risk Exposure | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sum Insured** | Maximum hospital bill paid per policy year | Underinsurance causes out-of-pocket loss | Minimum ₹15L-25L for Tier-1 family floater |
| **Room Rent Cap** | Per-day hospital room charge limit | Proportionate deduction on entire hospital bill | Choose **No Room Rent Cap** |
| **Co-Payment** | Fixed % paid out-of-pocket by policyholder | Increases out-of-pocket bill share | Choose **0% Co-Pay** for non-seniors |
| **Super Top-Up** | Policy triggering after base deductible | Low cost for high additional cover | Pair ₹5L Base Policy with ₹15L Top-Up |
| **Section 80D** | Tax deduction under Old Tax Regime | Max ₹1,00,000 statutory tax savings | Claim premium paid for self + senior parents |

### Step-by-Step Worked Example

Assume a 35-year-old individual planning for a nuclear family (Couple + 1 Child) in a Tier-1 Metro city:

1. **Coverage Demand Inputs**:
   - Baseline Tier-1 Critical Care Cost = ₹10,00,000
   - Medical Inflation = 12% p.a. over 5-Year Horizon ($10L \times 1.12^5 = ₹17.62L$)
   - Family Multiplier (Self + Spouse + 1 Child) = 1.45
   - **Recommended Total Health Cover** = **₹25.50 Lakhs** (Rounded to ₹25.50L)

2. **Employer Gap & Combination Strategy**:
   - Existing Corporate Employer Cover = ₹5,00,000
   - **Additional Retail Coverage Gap** = ₹25.50L − ₹5.00L = **₹20.50 Lakhs**
   - **Strategy**: Buy ₹5 Lakh Base Retail Policy + ₹20 Lakh Super Top-Up (₹5L Deductible)
   - Estimated Combined Premium = **~₹14,000/year** (vs ₹25,000/yr for single ₹25L base policy)

3. **Section 80D Tax Deduction (Old Tax Regime)**:
   - Self + Family Premium = ₹14,000 (Full deduction up to ₹25,000 limit)
   - Senior Citizen Parents Premium (62 Yrs) = ₹35,000 (Full deduction up to ₹50,000 limit)
   - **Total Section 80D Tax Savings @ 30% Tax Slab** = ₹49,000 × 30% = **₹14,700/year**
